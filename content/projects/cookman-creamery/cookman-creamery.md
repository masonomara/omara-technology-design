[cookmancreamery.com](https://cookmancreamery.com)

Cookman Creamery is celebrating their 10th year. Their website couldn't handle online orders and didn't capture who they were — an 1890s candy shop vibe with serious vegan offerings.

I ran a full design process: user research, three style directions presented for selection, content inventory, and mobile-first mockups. Built on Shopify with DoorDash integration, pickup scheduling, and custom cake ordering.

I created a brand guide documenting colors, typography, and gradients for consistency as they expand. The owners can now update products themselves. Customers order online, custom cakes work, and vegan options are easy to find.
