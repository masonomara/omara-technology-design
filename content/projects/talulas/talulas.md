[talulaspizza.com](https://talulaspizza.com)

Talula's is an Asbury Park pizza spot with strong branding — cartoon characters, custom type, a coral-and-cream palette. They needed a website that matched.

![Project planning document with site objectives, content requirements, and information architecture](/content/projects/talulas/images/define-project-1.png)
![Homepage design with brand graphics, menu links, and reservation button](/content/projects/talulas/images/mockups-1.png)

I built it on Shopify, letting their existing graphics do the heavy lifting while adding what a restaurant site needs: menus, events calendar, reservations through Resy, and online ordering through Toast. For merchandise, I set up print-on-demand integration so they don't carry inventory. Everything updates through Shopify admin.
