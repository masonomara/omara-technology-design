[interwovenapp.com](https://interwovenapp.com)

Interwoven is a boutique clothing store in Asbury Park known for bold merchandising and brand identity. They had a strong brand guide and 7,000+ SKUs, but no online presence to match. I built them a Shopify store that honored their visual identity while organizing inventory into logical categories by type, occasion, and collection.
The technical work involved replacing Shopify plugins with custom solutions aligned to their brand standards. I set up branded email automations and configured the platform for self-service content updates. The site launched as a revenue channel alongside their physical store.
