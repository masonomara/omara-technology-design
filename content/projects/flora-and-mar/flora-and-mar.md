[floraandmar.com](https://floraandmar.com)

Three products. The site needed to feel complete, not sparse.

Flora & Mar makes small-batch skincare and haircare with minimal, preservative-free ingredients. With only three products, they needed a site that felt complete rather than sparse — plus subscriptions, wholesale accounts, and an aesthetic matching their holistic approach.

I built the site on Shopify. We collaborated on design decisions together: engaging typography, botanical illustrations, and clean layouts that let the products breathe. Each product page lists exactly what's in the bottle. Subscriptions, wholesale locator, gift bundles.
