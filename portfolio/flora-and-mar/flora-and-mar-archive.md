[floraandmar.com](https://floraandmar.com)

Flora & Mar makes small-batch skincare and haircare with minimal, preservative-free ingredients. With only three products, they needed a site that felt complete rather than sparse, plus subscriptions, wholesale accounts, and an aesthetic matching their holistic approach.

![Flora & Mar product lineup showing Nourish Oil, Repair Balm, and Replenish Mist](/content/projects/flora-and-mar/images/screenshot-3.webp)

I built the site on Shopify. We collaborated on design decisions: engaging typography, botanical illustrations, and clean layouts that let the products breathe. Each product page lists exactly what's in the bottle. I set up subscription purchasing, a wholesale locator, and gift bundles. The site handles payments, inventory, and shipping without friction.

![Replenish Mist product page with ingredient list and subscription options](/content/projects/flora-and-mar/images/screenshot-2.webp)

![Botanical ingredient illustrations for the Replenish Mist](/content/projects/flora-and-mar/images/screenshot-1.webp)

The result is a fast, clean storefront that represents the brand well. Customers can subscribe easily, retailers find contact info quickly, and the design reflects the care that goes into the products.
