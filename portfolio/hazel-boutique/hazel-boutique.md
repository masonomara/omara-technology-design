[hazelboutique.com](https://hazelboutique.com)

Hazel Boutique is a New Jersey fashion retailer with four East Coast locations. They had a clear vision for their online store but needed a developer to build it properly and refine the design. I rebuilt their Shopify site from scratch during their brand overhaul.

![Mobile homepage and product collection views](/content/projects/hazel-boutique/images/showcase-2.webp)
![Desktop product detail page for crewneck sweatshirt](/content/projects/hazel-boutique/images/screenshot-2.png)

The build included marketing integrations for text and email campaigns, product features like favorites and reminders, inventory tools for multi-location operations, improved analytics, and third-party connections for shipping and performance tracking. The focus was editability — the e-commerce team needed to easily update the site for seasonal changes and new merchandise. Their team operates across shifting inventory and frequent sales, and the site supports that workflow.
